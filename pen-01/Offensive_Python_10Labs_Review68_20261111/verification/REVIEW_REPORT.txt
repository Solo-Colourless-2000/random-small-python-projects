WHAT THE ROUNDS MEAN
36 rounds are 36 machine-verifiable focuses. Every new focus reopens inspectable content files
(excluding cyclic reports and checksum manifests) and asserts a different property. These are not independent
professional manual reviews or exhaustive vulnerability evaluations.
Static source guards do not prove runtime safety against every possible input.
The app and its examples are intended for self-contained study only.

SAFETY LIMITATIONS
- 03 accesses 127.0.0.1 only.
- 04 remains a training comparison and does not close TOCTOU races.
- 05 uses an in-memory SQLite database; unsafe SQL must never be deployed.
- 06 has fictitious accounts and one simplistic global attempt limit.
- 07 has a public training key; HMAC validity is not application authorization.
- 09 reads a limited classic PCAP format, without full network validation.
- 10 only flags basic HTTP/1.1 framing ambiguity; does not decode chunks.
- A CLI PASS or VALID is not production security approval.
